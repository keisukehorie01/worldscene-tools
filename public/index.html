FAQ

Image to editable PowerPoint questions.

What does Drop2PPT do?

Drop2PPT converts AI-generated images, screenshots, diagrams, and mockups into editable PowerPoint PPTX slides.

Is the output just an image pasted into PowerPoint?

No. The goal is to rebuild text, shapes, panels, arrows, and layout as editable PowerPoint elements instead of placing a single flat image on a slide.

Who is this tool for?

It is for marketers, consultants, planners, creators, educators, and teams that need to turn visual drafts into editable presentation files.

Which image formats are supported?

The beta interface is prepared for PNG, JPG, and WebP images.
